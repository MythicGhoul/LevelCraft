# Clans, roles & territory claims

Clans are player organisations with managed membership, territory and protection. They provide long-term social goals and can be shown as region outlines on supported web maps.

## Typical clan lifecycle

1. Create a clan and invite friends.
2. Assign roles and manage clan membership.
3. Claim eligible chunks or territory.
4. Set a clan home and establish local rules.
5. Maintain claims and resolve territory conflicts.

## Clan functions

- Create, invite, join, kick, leave or disband.
- Promote and demote members.
- Claim/unclaim, inspect and list territory.
- Set/visit clan home and chat privately.
- View map representation and clan leaderboard data.

## Claim flags

Recent LevelCraft work added claim flags for **PvP** and **PvE**. Verify the actual flag defaults and how they interact with damage-cancellation plugins before opening your world to mixed combat styles.

## Key permissions

The referenced plugin manifest includes `lc.clan.use`, `lc.clan.create`, `lc.clan.invite`, `lc.clan.join`, `lc.clan.kick`, `lc.clan.leave`, `lc.clan.claim`, `lc.clan.unclaim`, `lc.clan.home`, `lc.clan.sethome`, `lc.clan.chat`, `lc.clan.map` and more. It also declares the elevated permission `lc.clans.admin.bypass`.

## Admin warning

Clan data influences build permissions and the corruption spawn exclusion radius. Any change to claim storage or map hooks should be tested for data integrity and unclaim side effects.
