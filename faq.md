# Frequently asked questions

??? question "Why is my Total Level not the sum of every skill?"
    The revised Total Level system uses an overall percentage/progression approach rather than simply adding starting skill levels. A newly created character with every skill at level 1 should show Total Level 1. See [Leveling](../progression/leveling.md).

??? question "Why does a high-rarity weapon feel weak?"
    Check the item's Power Level requirement. When the character is below the item requirement, damage and effectiveness can be reduced. See [Power Level](../equipment/power-level.md).

??? question "Can I change my race or path?"
    Admin reset workflows can permit reselection. Ordinary players should treat the initial choice as important unless their server has a specific respec policy.

??? question "Does vanilla XP equal LevelCraft XP?"
    No. Minecraft experience levels and LevelCraft skill XP are separate systems. Some features can also consume or grant vanilla experience.

??? question "Why are my quests not progressing?"
    Check the active quest's objective, target, eligibility and rank. In particular, custom drops, placed blocks and modified events can interact differently with quest counters. Ask staff to compare the objective with the server's quest definition.

??? question "Do I need a client mod?"
    Core LevelCraft is server-side. Servers may recommend an optional resource pack for specialised item textures.

??? question "Why aren't map markers showing?"
    Verify the relevant map plugin is installed, integration is enabled and that the world/marker actually exists. Some markers are registered at startup or after data loads.

??? question "Can players join parties and clans?"
    Yes, these are separate social systems. Parties help cooperative play and XP sharing; clans handle members, roles, territory and claims.
