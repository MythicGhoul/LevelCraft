# Economy, NPCs & custom items

LevelCraft's economy connects rewards to progression. Coins are awarded through gameplay and spent in shops, on upgrades and as quest prerequisites. An NPC network can include weapon, armour, tool, magic and resource merchants.

## Important distinction

The configuration manifest identifies **Vault as a required dependency**, but Vault is not itself a currency database. A compatible economy provider must also be present for purchases, prices and economy quest objectives to work.
