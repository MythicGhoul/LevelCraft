LevelCraft turns the Minecraft survival experience into a connected RPG. Develop **11 skills**, choose a **race and path**, complete **quests and promotion exams**, master **rarity equipment and magic**, and challenge an evolving world of **shrines, bosses and corruption**.

## 🌟 Core features

| Progression | World & combat | Social & economy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11 independent skills | Scaling mobs and boss encounters | Clans and land claims |
| 6 playable races | Corruption cores and guardians | Parties and shared progression |
| 6 character paths | Shrines and roaming encounters | NPC shops and coin economy |
| F → S guild ranks | Equipment rarity and sockets | Item trading and crafting systems |
| Quests, milestones and prestige | Spellcasting, wands and mana | Character profiles and leaderboards |

Detailed explanations, configuration examples and version notes are available in the [documentation directory](docs/README.md).
