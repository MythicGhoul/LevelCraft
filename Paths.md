# Character paths

Each player selects one path, representing their chosen approach to surviving and advancing in the world.

| Path | Focus | Suggested activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warrior** | Front-line combat | Defeating mobs and bosses |
| **Ranger** | Ranged precision | Exploring and hunting |
| **Mage** | Spell mastery | Learning and casting spells |
| **Craftsman** | Production and equipment | Mining, crafting and smithing |
| **Explorer** | Discovery and travel | Finding locations and new biomes |
| **Thief / Shadow** | Stealth-oriented versatility | Agile exploration and opportunistic encounters |

The sixth path was historically presented as **Thief** and later renamed **Shadow** in design discussions. The display name and internal ID should be verified in the running build rather than assuming they are interchangeable in admin commands.

## Selection and confirmation

- Compare the listed positive and negative modifiers in the sign or GUI interface.
- Recent builds include a roughly 12-second re-click confirmation for path choices.
- The selection may be mandatory before leaving an onboarding hall, depending on server settings.
- Path changes are ordinarily a staff-managed reset, not an unrestricted action.

## Placeholders

The PlaceholderAPI expansion includes `%levelcraft_path%`, `%levelcraft_rank_path%`, `%levelcraft_path_display%`, `%levelcraft_path_primary_skill%` and `%levelcraft_path_secondary_skill%`. See [Placeholders](https://github.com/MythicGhoul/LevelCraft/wiki/Reference-Placeholders).
