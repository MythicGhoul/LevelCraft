# Prestige, souls & endgame progression

Prestige extends character development after ordinary skill mastery. It is not the same as a guild promotion and is designed to reward sustained advancement.

## Prestige philosophy

Referenced LevelCraft designs use a score derived from leading skills rather than every skill equally. Earlier balancing discussed a **best-seven-skills** approach (excluding Fishing) and a **+25-level** increase per prestige tier for eligible progression. Because prestige was expanded across multiple updates, verify the live rule before publishing a permanent numeric requirement.

## Using prestige

Open the prestige interface with `/lc prestige`. Review the exact qualification threshold, rewards and any reset effects before confirming. The menu is authoritative for the specific server build.

## Prestige-related systems

- Prestige-linked XP boosts and other endgame benefits.
- Souls and soul restoration as additional survival progression resources.
- Ascension points and branches in the PlaceholderAPI implementation.
- Records of quests, bosses, cores and shrines that can contribute to long-term progression.

## Souls

Souls are a separate character resource connected to death protection and loss management. Servers can configure souls and grant them through administration; `/lc admin souls <player> <amount>` appears in a referenced command handler.

See [Placeholders](../reference/placeholders.md) for prestige, souls and ascension values.
