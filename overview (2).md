# Equipment and progression

Equipment has multiple independent dimensions. The material determines its vanilla base properties; **rarity** influences custom quality and bonuses; **Power Level** determines whether the character can use that equipment effectively; and **socket gems** add compatible specialised modifiers.

## How to evaluate an item

1. **Material and item type:** sword, bow, pickaxe, armour, tool or custom weapon.
2. **Rarity:** Common through Mastercraft.
3. **Power Level:** the requirement relative to your Total Level.
4. **Attributes and sockets:** which modifiers are present and which gem categories fit.
5. **Special behaviour:** custom items, bombs, scythes and magical tools may use additional rules.

## Why this matters

An item with better rarity but an excessive Power Level can be less useful to a low-level character than a correctly matched piece of gear. Compare real damage and survivability, not just the colour of the item name.
