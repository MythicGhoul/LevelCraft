# Guild ranks & promotion exams

Guild rank tracks your adventurer standing independently of ordinary skill levels. Rank progression follows **F → E → D → C → B → A → S**, beginning at F.

## The promotion flow

1. Open the quest menu and choose **Promotion Exams**.
2. Review the exam for the next guild rank.
3. Complete its prerequisites or questline steps.
4. Defeat the exam's configured boss or finish other promotion objectives.
5. Claim the promotion reward and check the updated rank.

The E-rank promotion boss is part of the existing progression; higher-rank exam encounters have also been developed/configured. Individual boss implementations and reward command lists vary by server data.

## Why rank and Total Level are separate

A player may reach a level high enough to wield certain equipment yet lack the required guild standing to access promotion-gated content. Conversely, a promoted adventurer still has individual skills to train.

## Troubleshooting a failed promotion

Check that the exam is active, that all questline steps are complete, that any required boss was defeated for the correct player or party, and that the claiming action occurred. An administrator should inspect [Quest configuration](../reference/quests-config.md) and any rank-reward commands before resetting progress.
