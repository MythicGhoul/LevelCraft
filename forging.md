# Forge and upgrades

LevelCraft's forging system gives players a route to enhance equipment beyond normal vanilla materials. The exact supported upgrade items, currency costs and chance curves are set by the live build.

## Basic workflow

1. Open the forge using `/lc forge` or an appropriately configured forge interface.
2. Place eligible LevelCraft equipment in the designated slot.
3. Check the currently shown recipe, payment, success chance and upgrade result.
4. Confirm the operation and retrieve the item.
5. Inspect the resulting rarity, Power Level, lore and socket information.

## Upgrade considerations

- The item must be recognised as LevelCraft equipment.
- A successful rarity upgrade may change its attributes and value.
- Some workflows require forge cores or other special resources.
- Never assume failed upgrades are free or harmless; check the live interface and configuration.
- Carry out testing with cloned gear on a development server.

## Relationship to smithing

The **Smithing skill** tracks crafting-oriented progression, while the **Forge system** handles custom equipment enhancement. They complement one another but are not identical.

See [Rarity](rarity.md), [Sockets](sockets.md) and [NPC shops](../economy/npc-shops.md).
