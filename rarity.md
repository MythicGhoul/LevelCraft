# Rarity tiers

Rarity describes the custom quality of an item. LevelCraft's published rarity ladder has six tiers:

| Tier | Position | Typical identity |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Common** | 1 | Standard entry-level custom gear |
| **Uncommon** | 2 | Early improvement |
| **Rare** | 3 | Stronger specialised equipment |
| **Epic** | 4 | High-end upgraded items |
| **Legendary** | 5 | Premium late-game equipment |
| **Mastercraft** | 6 | Highest published rarity tier |

## How rarity is applied

Rarity can affect dynamic item lore, damage or defence bonuses and forge progression. A conversion tool can transform ordinary eligible equipment into LevelCraft equipment while retaining existing enchantments in the referenced admin workflow.

### Equipment categories

Rarity applies to a broad item catalogue, including swords, armour and tools such as pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes and fishing rods. Custom scythes are a separate weapon family but still participate in rarity handling.

## Generated loot

Unconverted vanilla tools may appear in structure chests unless a loot conversion listener intercepts that source. Recent work added conversion for additional structure loot. See [Structures and loot](../world/shrines.md).

## Admin caution

The item **display name** alone is not the authoritative marker of LevelCraft rarity; internal metadata and custom lore matter. Never identify an item as converted solely because its name is coloured.
